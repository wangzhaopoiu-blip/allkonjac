Ingredients matched to your application

Konjac powder

Discuss the powder grade you need for your food product and production process. A useful enquiry includes the finished product, required specification and intended order quantity.

Refined konjac powder

For refined powder enquiries, share the purity and viscosity specification you require. Grade selection should be based on the specification of the proposed material and its performance in your own formulation.

Konjac gum

Konjac gum enquiries can be connected with hydrocolloid formulation and application development. Tell us the texture, water-holding or stability requirement your product needs to meet.

Prepare an ingredient enquiry

  • Finished food product and intended use.
  • Required purity, viscosity and any other specification, including test methods where applicable.
  • Current formulation and processing conditions you can share.
  • Sample or commercial quantity, packing requirements and destination market.
  • Documents required by your purchasing and quality teams.

Ask for the specification and documentation applicable to the proposed grade before placing an order. Qualification requirements depend on the ingredient, application and destination market.

Connect ingredients with development

If the material needs to be evaluated in a food formulation, discuss an application-development brief alongside the ingredient enquiry. This keeps ingredient selection connected to the finished product and its production process.

Project Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Which konjac ingredients can I enquire about?

You can enquire about food-grade konjac powder, refined konjac powder and konjac gum. Share your finished product and specification requirements so the discussion can focus on the appropriate grade.

How should I compare ingredient grades?

Compare the proposed specifications, including purity and viscosity with their test methods, and evaluate a sample in your intended formulation and process. Request the documents relevant to the proposed grade.

What should I send for a quotation?

Send the ingredient or application, required specification, quantity, packing requirements and destination market. If you need application development, include the texture or processing issue you want to address.
