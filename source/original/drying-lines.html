Konjac Dried-Chip Drying Lines

ALL KONJAC provides integrated drying-line solutions for konjac dried-chip processing. A line project brings together material preparation, conveying, drying equipment, air handling and process integration.

Discuss the complete drying process

Konjac drying-line enquiries can cover washing, slicing, conveying and drying stages. Temperature and humidity control, monitoring and the interfaces between equipment should be considered as part of the process brief.

Connected equipment modules

Project planning connects the drying chambers with conveying equipment, air-handling systems and drives. Define the material flow and the preparation and handling stages needed around the dryer.

Industrial project photography

The equipment photographs on this website show a real industrial drying environment, including conveying, drying equipment and air-handling components. Use them as a starting point for discussing layout and engineering scope.

Prepare a drying-line brief

  • Incoming material, slice dimensions and starting moisture specification.
  • Target dried-chip moisture and product requirements.
  • Expected input or output quantity and operating schedule.
  • Available building space, utilities and energy source.
  • Upstream preparation and downstream handling requirements.

Performance targets and utility requirements are project-specific. Confirm output, operating conditions and any energy targets in the technical proposal.

Engineering support

Discuss line design and equipment integration together with installation and commissioning support. The proposal should identify the machines, interfaces and start-up responsibilities included in the project.

Project Questions

Frequently asked questions.

What does a konjac drying-line project cover?

Depending on the project brief, the discussion can cover preparation, conveying, drying equipment, air handling, process control and line integration. Confirm the actual supply and engineering scope in the proposal.

How is the required dryer capacity determined?

Start with the incoming material specification, moisture requirements, expected input or output quantity and operating schedule. These project inputs need to be reviewed before equipment capacity is agreed.

Can I see real drying equipment photos?

Yes. This page and the homepage show industrial project photographs of drying equipment and production environments. Contact ALL KONJAC to discuss your layout and equipment requirements.
