Start with the application requirement

A development brief can address thickening, gel formation, texture, water holding or stability. Describe the issue in the actual food product rather than specifying an ingredient combination before the application has been reviewed.

Formulation development

Discuss the current formulation, ingredient constraints and desired result. The development scope can connect konjac ingredients with the product and the manufacturing method.

Application testing and sample development

Application work can include testing and sample development against the agreed product brief. Define how the samples will be assessed, including the handling, processing and storage conditions relevant to your project.

Prepare a technical enquiry

  • Food product and intended application.
  • Current texture, water-holding or stability issue.
  • Processing conditions and equipment constraints.
  • Ingredient and labelling requirements.
  • Target result, sample needs and evaluation criteria.

Project-specific formulation details and performance outcomes need evaluation. Share the information appropriate for an initial discussion and agree the development scope before proceeding.

Connect development to production

Formulation work can be discussed alongside ingredient selection, equipment planning and production-line integration. This helps connect the sample-development brief with the conditions in the factory.

Project Questions

Frequently asked questions.

What can a hydrocolloid development brief address?

A brief can address thickening, gel formation, texture improvement, water holding or stability. Include the food product and processing conditions so the requirement can be reviewed in context.

Is there one formulation for every konjac food product?

Formulation requirements depend on the food product, ingredients, process and target performance. Discuss and evaluate a project-specific solution rather than assuming one formulation suits every application.

What information is useful before sample development?

Share the application, current issue, processing conditions, ingredient constraints and evaluation criteria. Agree the target outcome and development scope before sample work begins.
